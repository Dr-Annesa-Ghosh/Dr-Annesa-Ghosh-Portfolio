✦ Case study 1 · Tag Media Works

Muthusaram Dental & Aesthetics

A dentist writing a dental clinic's Instagram. Muthusaram is a Chennai clinic with a dental side and a skin side. I'm a BDS graduate, so I knew which questions patients actually ask and which dental words lose them.

What I did

  • Built a monthly calendar split by department (dental, skin, both), listing the format, copy and caption for each post.
  • Wrote plain-language explainers on procedures: a carousel on the stages of a wisdom tooth, teeth-scaling before-and-afters, myth-busting posts, and tips on bad breath.
  • Designed a 14-day #SmileSquadChallenge to get followers posting their own routines.
  • Ended posts with one booking line and the clinic's number, so each one could turn into an appointment.
  • Read the Insights: short, funny, educational reels beat every other format, so I made more of them.

Results

+203.6%accounts reached in 30 days, across organic posts and paid boosts
+122.5%accounts engaged in the same 30 days
26Kplays on the "Smile magic!" reel, run as a boosted reel
72%of campaign leads became patients, as reported by the clinic

✦ Case study 2 · Tag Media Works

Tipsy Dive

A bar calendar built around theme nights, topical days and the IPL. Tipsy Dive is the bar at V Five Hotel in Kattankulattur, Chennai. A bar needs a reason to visit tonight, so I planned the feed around events people already cared about.

What I did

  • Built the weekly content rhythm around the bar's theme nights: Tamil Night on Sundays, Bollywood Night on Fridays, Club Night on Saturdays, and Ladies Night on Thursdays.
  • Tied posts to topical days (World Food Day, National Dessert Day, Pasta Day, Global Champagne Day) so there was a reason to post on quiet days.
  • For IPL season, planned a story for every match day, with special GIFs on CSK match days. The calendar tracked delivery and client approval for each piece.
  • Wrote reel briefs and designer notes, like using a cricket ball for the "O" in "We Never Run Out Of Options".
  • Reels with trending audio beat static posts in Insights, so I moved more of the plan to reels.

Results

+838.5%accounts reached in 30 days, across organic posts and paid boosts
+223.3%followers in the same 30 days, with accounts engaged up 174.8%
14Kplays on the Club Night reel, run as a boosted reel
37% · 51%rise in reservations and in signature-cocktail sales, as reported by the client

✦ Case study 3 · Tag Media Works

Two brands, two deliberately different systems.

Same person, different systems, each built for what the brand needed.

Madras Pickleball

Energy, in the city's own voice.

A pickleball club with courts in Thiruvanmiyur. I built the brand around bold blue and orange and one line, "Global Standards, Local Soul". The copy mixes Tamil and English the way Chennai actually talks.

Vanakkam Chennai! Semma rally time! Aatam aarambikkalama? Namma Chennai ready-aa?

The stories carry the offer and the next step: 12 coached classes a month for ₹12,000, or a 20-hour court pack for ₹12,000 to use within 30 days, booked on TurfTown or by phone.

House of Kade

Precision.

A minimalist fashion label. The visual system sets a colour rule: Champagne Gold, Cream White and Deep Charcoal, each with a set percentage of use, and never more than 3 colours in one post. The August calendar shows one outfit in 3 shots (half-length, close-up, full-length), with filler posts between.
